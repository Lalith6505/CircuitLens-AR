Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Intellectual Property & Cleanroom Privacy Standard

CircuitLens AR is engineered from the ground up for use in proprietary hardware engineering environments, confidential semiconductor research laboratories, cleanrooms, and industrial electronics facilities. We recognize that printed circuit board layouts, component revisions, and prototype hardware represent sensitive intellectual property. The application does not collect, record, or transmit any camera imagery, schematic data, or telemetry to external servers.

2. Camera & Hardware Sensor Access

The application requests runtime camera permission exclusively to project augmented reality pinout overlays onto physical components. Video frames are analyzed entirely in volatile device memory using real-time edge processing and are immediately discarded. No video streams or photographs are written to permanent storage unless the user explicitly triggers a local inspection export.

3. Zero Ads, Zero Trackers, Zero Third-Party SDKs

CircuitLens AR contains no third-party advertising frameworks, analytics trackers, behavioral diagnostics, or external crash-reporting libraries. The application does not contact any remote network host during normal execution.

4. Local Vault & Data Ownership

All inspection logs, board notes, and test records saved in the PCB Inspection Vault remain within the application's isolated local storage sandbox on the Android device. The user retains complete ownership of all data and can export or delete records at any time.

5. Policy Contact

For questions or architectural inquiries regarding CircuitLens AR, please contact the developer via the official Google Play Console developer page.
